The New Tesla "Flying Car": Next-Gen Roadster with SpaceX Thrusters

By Shahed October 22, 2026

Tesla has unveiled the next-generation Roadster, bridging the gap between electric supercars and rocket propulsion. Often dubbed in breaking headlines as the "Tesla Flying Car," the prototype combines downforce suction fans with high-pressure gas thrusters to achieve short-burst aerial hops and unprecedented acceleration.

Cold-Gas Thrusters and Ground-Effect Suction

Engineered in collaboration with SpaceX, the high-end Rocket configuration utilizes cold-gas nitrogen thrusters arranged around the perimeter of the vehicle. By releasing high-pressure gas bursts, the vehicle can lift momentarily off the ground, maintain balance, and dampen landing forces.

Simultaneously, electric ground-effect fans beneath the chassis generate immense downforce even while stationary, keeping the supercar securely planted during high-speed cornering at speeds reaching 230 mph.
